State-Of-The-Art Facilities

Computing Laboratories

Fully equipped computer labs to support practical development, software engineering, and AI experiments.

Lab 01

Software Engineering Lab

Dedicated lab equipped with high-performance PCs, modern IDEs, git version control, and database servers for undergraduate coding coursework.

  • 40 High-Speed Workstations
  • High-Speed Internet Backbone
Lab 02

AI & Data Science Lab

Specialized computing unit equipped with GPU workstations for training machine learning models, deep neural networks, and big data analysis.

  • NVIDIA GPU Accelerated Systems
  • Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow Suite
Lab 03

General Networks Lab

Equipped with Cisco networking hardware, routers, switches, and network simulators to provide hands-on experience in computer networking.

  • Hardware Racks & Switches
  • Packet Tracer & Wireshark Workstations
